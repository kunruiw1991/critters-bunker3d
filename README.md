## ✨ Game Highlights
Inspired by hilarious web-novel cozy bunker & dimensional pocket tropes (*"空间异能零元购 + 零下70度安全屋吃铜火锅 + 花花骑乐乐 + 吹笛子的大龙猫"*):
- **🌀 1:1 3D Purple Dimensional Vortex Vacuum (`空间异能漩涡`)**: Drag the glowing purple vortex ring across the 3D Supermarket Toy Shelf (or tap any food item) to suck **Tomahawk Ribeye Steaks**, **Giant Red Lobsters**, **Golden Swiss Cheese Wheels**, **Strawberry Shortcakes**, **Bouncy Fishball Skewers**, and **Sweet Corn Cobs** along a `1/120s` substepped 3D spiral trajectory into your cozy bunker tray!
- **🍲 Bubbling 3D Copper Mandarin-Duck Hotpot (`鸳鸯铜火锅`)**: Drop supplies into the twin Spicy Red & Savory Gold boiling broths (`RingGeometry` vertex-displaced waves) and serve steaming delicacies to hungry Smiling Critters!
- **🥣 3D Sesame & Chili Dipping Sauce Bar (`sauce_bar`)**: Tap the twin ceramic sauce bowls (or drag a cooked dish through them) to glaze any hotpot dish with a rotating 3D 4-star golden halo (`pot.glazed = true`), awarding `+18` bonus coins in Wave 1 (`+22` in Waves 2 & 3) and `+1` extra combo pip!
- **☕ 3D Cozy Marshmallow Cocoa Mug Warmer (`cocoa_station`)**: Tap the glowing copper mug warmer to launch a 3D marshmallow-topped cocoa mug along a parabolic flight arc to the lowest-patience active guest, restoring `+6.0s` patience and awarding `+15` bonus coins!
- **🐷🎁 3D Sous-Chef Helper Buddy (`helper_buddy`) & Surprise Golden Honey-Pot Crate (`golden_crate`)**: `Picky Piggy` in a white chef toque automatically assists every `5.5s` (loading needed tray ingredients or glazing & serving ready hotpots before they burn) and enters a 4-second `2.5x` turbo mode when tapped! Mid-wave, a shimmering Golden Honey-Pot Crate parachutes from the skylight—tap or sweep the vortex over it to burst 8 golden stars, award `+25` bonus coins, restore `+4.0s` patience to all active guests, and auto-glaze active hotpots!
- **🌈👑 Stage-by-Stage Wave 1, Wave 2 & Wave 3 Visual & Toy Evolutions**:
  - **Wave 1 (`cozy_amber_cabin`)**: Warm amber fireplace glow, snow-frosted skylight, and full 3-tier zero-dead-tap toy stations.
  - **Wave 2 (`aurora_ice_lantern_party`)**: Waving 3D Aurora Borealis ribbon curtain outside the window, twin 3D Crystal Ice Lanterns on the cabin table, `+22` double-dip sauce glaze bonus, and 3D `Bubba Bubbaphant` plush guest in Seat 1.
  - **Wave 3 (`grand_golden_royal_banquet`)**: 8-bulb Festive Fairy String Lights across the timber beam, Golden Dragon-Crown Hotpot Rim on both copper pots, spinning overhead Party Disco Star Ball, 3D `Kickin Chicken` & `Bobby BearHug` VIP plush guests, and Royal Chinchilla Flute upgrade (dual golden + magenta shockwave rings + automatic golden sauce glazing on active hotpots).
- **🐱🐶 CatNap Riding DogDay (`花花骑乐乐`) & 🐹 Flute-Playing Giant Chinchilla (`吹笛子的大龙猫`)**: Features the authentic `v11` 3D plush CatNap mounted on `v11` DogDay's back, plus a fluffy 3D Giant Chinchilla playing a golden bamboo flute that sends out musical shockwaves to speed-boost cooking and cheer up guests!
- **⛄ Frosted `-70°C` Window Snowball Defense**: Bonk peeking `Baba Chops` snow-bandits outside the frosted window into hilarious 3D Snowmen with carrot noses!
- **🔇 100% Zero-Text & Zero-Voiceover Pure Visual UI**: Designed for 5-year-old players with pure 3D visual cues (`alphaHanziCount === 0`, `speechSynthesis === 0`), custom procedural WebAudio ASMR (`playSauceSizzle`, `playCocoaSip`, `playHelperChirp`, `playGoldenCrateFanfare`, `playWaveEvolutionChime`), Non-IBL Three.js r161 material hygiene (`maxMetalness <= 0.22`), and a 5-Critter Top-3 Podium progression system.
